### General Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**  
The working environment here is very friendly, cheerful, and welcoming. The workspace is absolutely wonderful, dynamic, and amazing, giving me a lot of inspiration and comfort to complete my work effectively.

**2. Support from Mentors / Team Admins**  
The mentors and team admins are incredibly enthusiastic and dedicated. Whenever I have any questions or need guidance, they are always ready to answer immediately, while fully supporting me in every way possible. Furthermore, they constantly encourage and remind me to keep striving forward.

**3. Alignment between Work and Major**  
This internship has helped me acquire a lot of valuable new knowledge. Combining these new learnings with the foundational knowledge I gained at university has allowed me to consolidate and enhance numerous practical skills.

**4. Opportunities for Learning & Skill Development**  
Working at the company has given me vast hands-on experience. Not only have I been exposed to professional real-world workflows, but I have also significantly improved my problem-solving mindset and developed a much more polished and professional work ethic than before.

**5. Culture & Teamwork**  
The strong bond among everyone during my internship is something I felt very clearly. There is absolutely no gap between anyone; everyone listens to, respects each other's opinions, and works together toward the common goal of the team.

**6. Recommendation to Friends**  
I will definitely recommend and advise my friends to intern here. It is an ideal environment for students—a place where you get to work in a fantastic workspace alongside highly enthusiastic mentors who help you grow and become much more confident.
