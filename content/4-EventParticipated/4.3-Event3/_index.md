# Event Report(participate online)

The "FCAJ Community Day - June 2026" event was an in-depth technology forum focusing on the application of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in enterprise operations, from Cloud infrastructure, voice customer support (Voice AI), DevOps processes, to Human Resources (HR) management and system security. The event was highly practical with many presentations from experts directly working at large technology enterprises and startups.

### Event Objectives

The FCAJ Community Day event is a monthly activity organized to create a community networking space. The core purpose is for speakers from various enterprises to share their knowledge, experience, and the most practical perspectives originating from the corporate working environment. The event was held simultaneously offline (on the 26th and 36th floors) and livestreamed on YouTube to reach a large audience.

### Key Highlights

The event was divided into 4 highly practical technology topics:

#### 1. Career Orientation & AI in Cloud Operations

Mr. Steve Tran shared his journey from a student to a Founder, while emphasizing how AI is changing recruitment needs. Current AI tools greatly assist in handling complex system architectures, cost optimization (FinOps), and security automation. He also deeply discussed the superiority of Single-agent architecture over Multi-agent in optimizing cost and performance when solving infrastructure tasks.

#### 2. Voice AI Agent

Exploring the potential of Voice AI in Vietnam. Mr. Trung (R AI) explained the reason for not using a direct Speech-to-Speech model for Vietnamese, but rather a conversion architecture: Speech -> Text -> LLM -> Text -> Speech to strictly control the content the AI speaks and support "tool calling" features (e.g., automatically locking a bank card). Vietnamese contextual issues such as gender distinction, forms of address, regional accents, and the skill of "interrupting" were also delved into.

#### 3. DevOps AI Agent

The Cloud Kinetics team introduced a solution using AI as a system error analysis assistant. Instead of engineers having to dive into logs in many places, the DevOps AI Agent automatically synthesizes, investigates the Root Cause Analysis, and proposes a Mitigation plan. This helps significantly reduce the Mean Time To Recovery (MTTR), but the final decision-making power still belongs to humans.

#### 4. Amazon Q for Enterprise & Security

- **Application for HR**: Solves the problem of manual, subjective CV reading and data security risks. AI tools can automatically extract information, cross-check CVs with JDs, score skills, and create detailed HR reports.
- **Connection Security**: To ensure data safety, Mr. Toan Nguyen presented the architecture of connecting AI with third-party servers (MCP Server) through VPC Connection and Private Subnet, helping internal data not be exposed to the public Internet.

### What Was Learned

- **The Shift in the Job Market**: AI does not completely replace humans, especially in critical production operations environments. However, the market will stop widespread hiring for entry-level positions, and shift towards looking for truly excellent senior engineers, or those who know how to use AI proficiently to increase productivity.
- **Mindset for Problem Solving with AI**: When building AI, it's not always best to use the latest technology. For example, with Vietnamese (a low-resource language), breaking down the model (converting to Text for LLM processing) brings higher accuracy and customization capabilities.
- **Security is Vital**: Applying AI in large enterprises is not just about "what AI can do", but also "does AI have a secure connection". Setting up Private VPCs to protect data flows is crucial infrastructure knowledge.
